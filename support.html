Common questions

How do I open Settings or the parent report?
Tap the gear on the home screen and answer the grown-ups question.

Will my child see the same question again?
No. Every question is new for each child. If a word bank runs low for a grade, the app says so instead of repeating.

How do I add or change a child?
In Settings, open App, then Children. You can add up to four children.

How do I cancel a subscription?
On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions.

How do I delete my account?
Settings → Delete account. This deletes every child profile and all practice data right away. You can also email us from your account address and we will delete it within 7 days.

Is this the real CogAT®?
No. GiftedPrep is independent practice with original questions in the same formats.
