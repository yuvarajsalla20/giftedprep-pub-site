🦉 GiftedPrep

Privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

GiftedPrep is a practice app for children, run by their parents. We collect as little as we can, we never show ads, and we never sell or share personal information for advertising. This policy explains what we keep and why.

Who has the account

Only a parent or legal guardian can create a GiftedPrep account. Children do not sign up, do not give an email address, and cannot message anyone. Parent areas (settings, reports, purchases and account deletion) sit behind a grown-ups check.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Parent account: email address, password (stored hashed by our sign-in provider), an optional nickname, and the time zone and report settings you choose.To sign you in, keep your children's progress, and send the daily report email if you turn it on.
Child profile: a first name or nickname, grade, an optional test date and school district, and the buddy and color the child picks.To pick the right questions for the grade and show a countdown to the test.
Practice results: which questions were shown, which answer was picked, whether it was right, and how long it took.To never repeat a question, to suggest what to work on next, and to build the progress report, streaks, stars and badges.
Crash reports: if the app crashes, a technical report (device model, OS version, app version and the error). We remove names and emails from these reports before they are sent.To find and fix bugs.

We do not collect a child's photo, voice, location, contacts, or device advertising ID. Read-aloud uses your device's built-in voice, and no audio leaves the device. We do not use advertising or tracking tools.

In preview mode (before you sign in), everything stays on your device and nothing is sent to us.

Who processes the data

We use a small number of service providers, only to run the app:

  • Supabase stores accounts and practice data, with access rules so each parent sees only their own children.
  • Sentry receives crash reports.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers the daily report email, if you turn it on.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play handle purchases. We never see your card details.

They may use the data only to provide their service to us.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

GiftedPrep is designed for children under 13 and follows the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. A parent creates the account and adds each child, which is how we get parental consent. We collect only what the app needs, as listed above. A parent can review, change or delete a child's information at any time: edit or remove the child in Settings, or delete the whole account.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. Removing a child deletes that child's profile and practice history. Deleting your account (Settings → Delete account) permanently deletes the account, every child profile and all practice data right away. Crash reports are deleted automatically within 90 days.

Your choices and rights

  • See and change your children's information in the app at any time.
  • Turn the daily report email on or off in Settings.
  • Delete your account in the app, or write to us and we will do it for you.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data.

Depending on where you live (for example California or the EU/UK), you may have more rights, such as access, correction, deletion and objection. Write to us and we will help.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and database rules make sure each family can only reach its own data. No system is perfect, and we will tell you if a breach affects your data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell parents in the app or by email before the change takes effect.
