🦉 GiftedPrep

Gifted & Talented test practice for K–6

GiftedPrep gives children short daily practice in the nine kinds of questions on CogAT®-style tests: verbal, quantitative and nonverbal. Every question is new, so a child never sees the same one twice. Kindergartners get picture questions that are read aloud.

  • About 10 minutes a day, with a daily mission and streaks
  • Practice tests with the real number of questions and timing
  • A progress report for parents, behind a grown-ups check
  • No ads. We never sell data.
